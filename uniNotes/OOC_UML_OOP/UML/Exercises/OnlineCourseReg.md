# Project Objectives:

Make a system that handles enrollment into online courses by students.
This system should give functionalities to instructors and academic staff.

# Scope of Work:

This system will be utilized exclusively by students, instructors and academic staff.

# Functional Requirements:

This system should allow students to enroll into courses, check the course material as well as the timetables set for instructor-led courses. They must also be provided with the ability to give feedback on a course and/or professor.
While instructors should be capable of creating timetables for their courses, upload course material to the platform as well as be able to give feedback on students.
Academic staff will mainly handle the tracking of student and professor data, as well as reviewing the courses made by instructors.
