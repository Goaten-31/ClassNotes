# Project Objectives:

Build a hospital system that keeps patient care records accurate and accessible. It benefits patients, doctors and administrative staff.

# Scope of Work:

Target users are hospitals, specifically their doctors and administrative staff. Patients benefit from complete records but do not use the system directly.

# Functional Requirements:

- Store each patient's ID, name, age and medical history.
- Store doctors and their specialties.
- Let a doctor treat many patients and a patient consult many doctors.
- Record each consultation's patient, doctor, date and diagnosis.
- Allow a consultation to have one optional prescription containing medications.
- Show a patient's past consultations as part of their medical history.

**Added:**

- Calculate age from birth date.
- Schedule, reschedule and cancel appointments without double-booking doctors.
- A completed appointment links to its consultation. Walk-in consultations need no appointment.
- Record dosage, frequency and duration for each prescribed medication.
- Store allergies and chronic conditions.
- Warn when a prescribed medication matches a recorded allergy.
