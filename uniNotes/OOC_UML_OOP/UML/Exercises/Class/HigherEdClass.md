# Project Objectives:

Build a system to manage a higher education school's students, teaching and results. It benefits students, teachers and administrators.

# Scope of Work:

Target users are higher education schools, specifically their students, teachers and academic administrators.

# Functional Requirements:

- Enroll each student in one program.
- Organize programs into semesters, teaching units and courses.
- Assign each course to one department.
- Assign every teacher at least one course.
- Let students take several courses.
- Record students' grades and let them view results.
