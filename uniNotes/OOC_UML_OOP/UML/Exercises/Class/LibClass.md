# Project Objectives:

Computerize a public library to improve efficiency and reduce manual work. It benefits members and staff.

# Scope of Work:

Target users are public libraries, their members and staff.

# Functional Requirements:

- Maintain books (title, authors, ISBN, category, total and available copies), authors (full name, nationality), and member details (ID, contacts, membership validity).
- Staff register members and authors, manage authors, and add, edit or remove books and categories.
- Let valid members borrow books within a limit.
- Record borrowing, expected-return and actual-return dates; update availability automatically.
- Detect overdue loans, calculate possible fines and alert staff.
- Report borrowed books, overdue items and popular categories.
- Let members search by title, author or category and view their borrowing history and due dates.
