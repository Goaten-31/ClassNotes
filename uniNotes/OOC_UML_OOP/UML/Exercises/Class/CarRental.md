# Project Objectives:

Build a system that organizes vehicle rentals efficiently. It benefits customers, employees and company managers.

# Scope of Work:

Target users are vehicle rental companies, their customers and employees.

# Functional Requirements:

- Manage cars, motorcycles and trucks by registration, model, mileage, condition, availability and maintenance history.
- Let customers view available vehicles, request rentals for a period and review past contracts.
- Contracts record start date, end date, total price, customer and vehicle.
- An employee manages and validates each contract after checking customer information and vehicle status.
- Calculate price from duration and vehicle type.
- Check vehicles before and after rental; update availability at handover and return.
- Employees register vehicles, update conditions, manage customers and process payments.
- Report active rentals, vehicle usage and company performance.
- Approve requests to create contracts.
- Prevent overlapping reservations and maintenance bookings.
- Record inspections, mileage and damage.
- Add late fees.
- Require payment before handover.
- Verify driving license validity and category.
